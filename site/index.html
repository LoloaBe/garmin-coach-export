Fatigue (ATL, 7-day)
Banister impulse-response model (the same math behind TrainingPeaks' PMC) — a widely used heuristic for tracking the fitness/fatigue balance, not a validated predictor of performance. The first ~5-6 weeks of the fitness line are still ramping up because the model only has this 90-day window to learn from; read the trend, not the early absolute level.
Training stress balance (form)
—
Positive = fresh (fitness exceeding recent fatigue), negative = carrying fatigue. Useful for spotting whether a taper is landing, not for daily decisions.
Acute:chronic workload ratio
—
7-day load ÷ 28-day average daily load. Shaded band (0.8–1.3) is the commonly cited "sweet spot" from team-sport injury research (Gabbett et al.) — evidence in endurance sport specifically is thinner, so treat >1.5 as a prompt to watch recovery, not a hard limit.
